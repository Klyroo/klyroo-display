# Lux.Display Skill

- Use `LxDisplay` with `DisplayKind` (`Heading`, `Text`, `Alert`, `Facts`, `Stat`, `Breakdown`, `Meter`, `Chart`, `Table`, `Diff`, `Record`, `Person`, `List`, `Timeline`, `Date`, `Media`, `Code`, `Empty`, `Accordion`).
- Use `LxAudioPlayer` and `LxVideoPlayer` for clips (`Start`/`End`), playback limits (`MaxPlays`/`Plays`), and non-seekable media.
- Use `FilePreviewService` and `LxFilePreviewProvider` to preview PDF, images, video, audio, CSV, Markdown, JSON/code, and Office renditions.
